## Boundaries of the construction

The realized continuum branch is defined directly, so its receiving descriptions need no unproved discretization-to-continuum reconstruction. Ordinary continuum analysis remains in use. The proved rest-subtracted nonrelativistic limit and local observation comparisons are separate analytic limits.
